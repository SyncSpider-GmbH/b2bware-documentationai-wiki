---
title: Edit a product
description: Update General, Price, and Stock tabs on a product in B2Bware Admin.
---

Open a product from **All Products**, then use the tabs to maintain identity, pricing, and inventory.

## General

<Image
  src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-d7f30541-cdff-445d-a83f-48be426e0029/doc-ab18cb81-c197-4682-883d-2e8d54dd7f3f/1789404825814-w0bijm3rty-detail-general.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat"
  alt="Edit Product — General tab"
/>

Typical General fields include name, SKU, description, categories, and media.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product">
    From **All Products**, select the product row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit General">
    Update name, description, and other identity fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or reorder photos">
    Upload images and set the primary photo when your tenant supports it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save before switching tabs if your UI requires an explicit save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" title="Reorder photos">
  Drag thumbnails when the UI allows it so the first image is the one buyers see first.
</Callout>

## Price

<Image
  src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-d7f30541-cdff-445d-a83f-48be426e0029/doc-ab18cb81-c197-4682-883d-2e8d54dd7f3f/1789404829280-g3qw7ceri8r-detail-price.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat"
  alt="Edit Product — Price tab"
/>

Use the Price tab for base price and any list- or contract-related fields your tenant shows.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Variants prices label">
  Some tenants show a label such as **Vatiants Prices** (spelling as in the UI). Use that section for variant-level prices when products have variants.
</Callout>

## Stock

<Image
  src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-d7f30541-cdff-445d-a83f-48be426e0029/doc-ab18cb81-c197-4682-883d-2e8d54dd7f3f/1789404832291-noqwqvkbe5-detail-stock.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat"

Assign quantities per stock location. Create locations first under **Stock Locations** if the list is empty.

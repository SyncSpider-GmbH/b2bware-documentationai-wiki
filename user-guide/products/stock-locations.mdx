## Open the module

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand Products">
    In the left sidebar, expand **Products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Stock locations">
    Select **Stock locations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" title="Used on product Stock">
  Product Stock tabs list these locations. Add a location here first if it is missing on a product.
</Callout>

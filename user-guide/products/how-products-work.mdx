**Products** owns the sellable catalog in B2Bware Admin: products (including configurable variations), categories, multi-warehouse stock, multi-type pricing and deployable pricelists, vendors, and related media/translations.

Use this page to understand the system. Day-to-day screens are covered in the existing Products how-tos linked at the end.

## What Products owns (sidebar)

When Products is installed and you can view it, the sidebar typically includes:

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **All Products** | Create and maintain products — types, variants, attributes, stock/price tabs, SEO, cross-sell/upsell, and more |
| **Categories** | Hierarchical catalog structure |
| **Stock locations** | Warehouses and stock per product/location |
| **Pricelists** | **Staged** price sets — live catalog prices change when you **deploy** |
| **Vendors** | Supplier / vendor records and product links |
| **Customer SKUs** | Buyer-specific part numbers — only fully active when the optional Customer SKU module is installed |

Products is a **core** hub (part of the commerce spine with Orders, Customers, Auth, and Settings). See [How B2Bware fits together](/user-guide/how-b2bware-fits-together).

## Dependencies

| Related hub / module | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Attributes** | Dynamic product attributes and variation attributes for configurables |
| **Customers** | Group prices (customer groups / Segmentation); product permission tabs when those modules are installed |
| **Orders** | Cart and checkout consume products and resolved prices |
| **Rules** (optional) | Catalog/cart promotions stack on top of base / provider prices |
| **Media** | Product and category media |
| **Tax** (optional) | Tax class on products when Tax is installed |
| **Customer SKU module** (optional) | Buyer SKUs and order-line snapshots — do not treat as always-on core |
| **Integrations / Import-Export** | Catalog sync and bulk upsert — separate UI from Product Hub |

## Install and permissions

- **Install:** Product Hub must be installed on the tenant.
- **Sidebar:** Visible when installed **and** your role can view Product Hub modules.
- **Fine-grained admin permissions** cover products, categories, warehouses/stock, prices, pricelists (including **deploy**), vendors, media, translations, attributes, and related actions (import/export, bulk update, own-record views where configured).

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
Shop-facing catalog permission labels (for example “view prices” / “view stock”) may exist in the permission model but are **not fully enforced** in all shop journeys today. Buyer visibility is often driven by **tenant settings** (such as requiring login for prices). Do not document those shop keys as a guaranteed ACL yet.
</Callout>

## How pricing is meant to work

Operators should distinguish three layers:

1. **Live product prices** — regular, special (with dates), tier (quantity), group (customer group), and cost.
2. **Final price cascade (base)** — typically tier → group → special → regular, unless a server-side **customer price provider** (from integrations) supplies a trusted unit price first.
3. **Pricelists** — prepare many price rows offline, then **deploy** onto live Price records. Editing a pricelist alone does not change what buyers pay until deploy.

Rules (if installed) can still adjust outcomes on top of that base. Themes and clients must never invent charged prices — resolution is **server-authoritative**.

## Product types (supported model)

Documented product types include **simple**, **configurable** (variations), **virtual**, and **downloadable**. Do not invent additional types in the User Guide without product confirmation.

## Do not claim

- Customer SKUs always work without the Customer SKU module installed.
- Optional detail tabs (for example licensing or top-up related UIs) are required Product Hub features — they appear when those optional apps are in scope.
- Shop catalog permission keys are fully enforced for every storefront journey.

## What you'll learn

- Open **Customer SKUs** under **Products**
- Understand the purpose: map customer-specific SKUs
- Add a mapping with **Add Customer SKU**

## Open Customer SKUs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand Products">
    In the left sidebar, expand **Products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Customer SKUs">
    Select **Customer SKUs**. The screen purpose is to *Map customer-specific SKUs*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Customer SKUs](../../screenshots/products/customer-skus.png)

## Search and filters

On **Customer SKUs** you can:

- Use **Search** to find mappings
- Filter by **Owner type**

An empty workspace shows an empty state until mappings exist.

## Add a customer SKU

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Customer SKU">
    Select **Add Customer SKU**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the mapping">
    Enter the customer-specific SKU details and **Owner type** as shown on the form, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" title="When to use Customer SKUs">
  Use this module when buyers order with their own SKU codes and you need those codes mapped to your catalog products.
</Callout>

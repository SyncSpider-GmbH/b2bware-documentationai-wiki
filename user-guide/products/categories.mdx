## Open the module

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand Products">
    In the left sidebar, expand **Products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Categories">
    Select **Categories**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" title="Product Count">
  The product count on a category reflects how many products are assigned. Reassign products on the product General tab if a count looks wrong.
</Callout>

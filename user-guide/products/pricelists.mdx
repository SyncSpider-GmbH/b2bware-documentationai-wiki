---

Use pricelists when buyers should see contract or tier prices instead of a single list price.

## Open the module

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand Products">
    In the left sidebar, expand **Products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Pricelists">
    Select **Pricelists**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warning" title="Deploy carefully">
  Changing an active pricelist can affect live buyer prices. Confirm with ops before large edits.
</Callout>

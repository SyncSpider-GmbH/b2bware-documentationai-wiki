## Open All Products

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Products hub">
    In the left sidebar, expand **Products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose All Products">
    Select **All Products** to open the catalog list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find a product

- Use the search field for name, SKU, or other indexed fields
- Use column filters when the list is long
- Sort by clicking a column header when available

<Callout kind="info" title="Attribute columns">
  Extra attribute columns may appear depending on your tenant setup. Treat them as filters for your catalog, not as required fields for every product.
</Callout>

## Create a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start create">
    Click **Create** (or the equivalent primary action) on All Products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter basics">
    Fill in at least name and SKU (and any required fields your tenant shows).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the product, then continue on the product detail tabs for media, price, and stock.
  </Step>
</Steps>
